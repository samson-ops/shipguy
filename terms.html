1. About Ship Guy — Broker Role

Ship Guy is a licensed motor-carrier property broker (FMCSA MC #1457715 · DOT #3932149) that arranges the transportation of vehicles between shippers and independent, third-party motor carriers.

Ship Guy is not a motor carrier and does not physically transport, load, unload, or take possession of any vehicle. The actual transportation is performed by independent carriers that are separately licensed and insured. Ship Guy does not own or operate the trucks or equipment used to transport your vehicle and does not employ the drivers. Ship Guy is not an agent of any carrier, and no carrier is an agent of Ship Guy.

The BOL is the primary record of the vehicle's condition. You should carefully inspect your vehicle at delivery and note any new damage on the BOL before signing. Signing the BOL without noting damage may limit your ability to make a claim.
